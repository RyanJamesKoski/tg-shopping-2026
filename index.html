🦃 Thanksgiving Shopping

Route (~27 mi, ~52 min driving): Target → ALDI → Walmart → BJ's → Publix → Harris Teeter → home. Turkey is last so it stays frozen. Open the whole route in Google Maps.
Before you go: cooler · quarter for ALDI cart · Target Circle in the Target app · BJ's card + clip the BJ's coupons in the app · Harris Teeter VIC card (free, phone #) + clip the e-VIC Grands deal.
Target prices include the team member 10% + Circle Card 5%. Every item is at whichever of these 6 stores has the best current price or sale. Aisle numbers aren't posted online for these stores, so each item shows its section instead.

Want to skip a store? Extra cost if you skip it:
• BJ's: +$0.65 (just the dairy-free butter → Walmart). Easy skip.
• Publix: +$5.67 (Pie crusts → ALDI; Cherry filling → Walmart)
• Harris Teeter: +$10.36 (Turkey → Target G&G $0.79/lb; Grands → Walmart; Chocolate → ALDI)
• Target: +$10.87 (Ham → Harris Teeter; OJ → Harris Teeter; Cheese → ALDI; Bacon → ALDI; Stock → Walmart; Sausage → ALDI)
• Walmart: Target probably carries the same stuff (plant cream, CocoWhip, Rhodes, etc.). With your 15% off, if Target's shelf price is close to the Walmart price listed, grab it at Target and skip Walmart.
• ALDI: keep — it's most of the list and the cheapest.
